Hi. My name is Philipp Rieber.

I’m a passionate software engineer. My tools are PHP, Symfony, Doctrine, HTML5, JavaScript, CSS3, Twig, Git, Amazon Web Services, databases and many more.

I live in Munich, Germany.

Internet

With my first email in 1999 the internet became my second home. After some hardware tinkering and first steps on the Linux console, I really became obsessed with web development while studying the selfHTML tutorials. After that, I’ve never stopped learning new technologies: HTML, CSS, PHP, JavaScript, databases, Flash, CMS, OOP, frameworks, servers, Git, AWS, Scrum, cloud services, mobile and many more.

Software development

I’m a full stack software engineer. I mainly develop fully customized web applications and APIs, and I focus on DevOps tasks. Thus, I’m not limited to a specific area and feel equally comfortable on the client and the server side – as well as on the console, during software design stage, in a Scrum team or when exploring a customer’s precise needs.

Jobs

I’m working as a Senior Software Engineer at ottonova, the first digital private health insurance in Germany. Before that, I worked at Paymill (payments) and at Inviqa (agency). I evaluated an InsurTech startup idea at WERK1 and worked for seven years as a web developer, team lead and deputy managing director of an internet agency (Onemedia GmbH). I also advise and support a few other projects.

About me

Besides software development, I write and talk about it and like to support other developers. Every free minute I try new tools and libraries, read documentation, do tutorials, check IT news or watch video trainings. I love to constantly learn new things and to improve my skills.

Certifications

  • SensioLabs Certified Symfony Developer (Expert)
  • Zend Certified PHP Engineer

Conferences

I love to attend software development conferences and workshops. For example, I attended Symfony Live and the Symfony Day several times, as well as some roadshows, presentations or trainings from Adobe, Amazon and Microsoft.

User groups

I am co-organizing the Symfony User Group Munich and I rarely miss a meetup of the PHP User Group Munich. I’m also an occasional speaker at these user groups.

Contributions

On GitHub, I’m maintaining small open source libraries and bundles. From time to time I contribute to other projects, preferably for the Symfony documentation. I also like to participate in discussions on GitHub, StackOverflow or various blogs and forums.

Publications

Every now and then I write articles and books about software development or translate from English. In everyday work I’m also writing a lot for case studies, references or documentation. Occasionally I blog at blog.philipp-rieber.net.
