Visit statistics

I count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly open-source analytics service. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Your browser sends the visited page, the referrer and technical details such as screen width and browser type to GoatCounter, which derives your country from your IP address.

According to GoatCounter’s privacy policy, it does not store IP addresses and keeps only aggregate counts. To recognize repeat visits, it holds your IP address and browser information in memory for up to eight hours. It is operated from Ireland on servers in Finland and Germany and shares no information with third parties. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing how this site is used (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). You can object at any time by email or block the script; the site works the same without it.
