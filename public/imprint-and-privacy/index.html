Visit statistics

This website uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly open-source web analytics service, to count visits. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. When a page is opened, your browser sends the visited page, the referrer and technical details such as screen width and browser type to GoatCounter. The approximate location is derived from your IP address.

According to GoatCounter’s privacy policy, it does not store IP addresses and keeps only aggregate counts that cannot be linked to individual visitors. To recognize repeat visits, it holds a combination of IP address and browser information in memory for at most eight hours. The service is operated from Ireland on servers in Finland and Germany, and no information is shared with third parties.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing how this site is used (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). You can object at any time by emailing me, or you can block the script with your browser or a content blocker; the site works the same without it.
