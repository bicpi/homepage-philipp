Google Analytics

This website can use Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service of Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) and Google LLC (USA) (“Google”), to count visits. Analytics is off by default. It is only loaded after you click “Allow” in the notice at the bottom of the page. Until then, no connection to Google is made and no analytics cookies are set.

If you allow it, Google Analytics uses cookies to recognize your browser and to analyze how the site is used, for example which pages are viewed, the approximate location, and the device and browser type. According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses. The information may be transmitted to Google and stored on servers outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. Where this happens, the transfer relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or standard contractual clauses. On behalf of the operator of this website, Google uses the information to evaluate the use of the site and to compile reports on website activity.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR and § 25 (1) TDDDG). Your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage (entry “analytics-consent”) and is not sent to any server. You can change or withdraw it at any time with “Analytics settings” in the footer. Declining after having allowed analytics removes the analytics cookies from this site. A withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of the processing before it. You can also prevent the collection of data by Google with the browser add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

More information on terms of use and data protection: policies.google.com/privacy.
